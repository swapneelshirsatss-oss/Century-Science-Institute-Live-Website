# Research synthesis: what students and parents say about Century Science Institute

**Method:** desk research of public reviews | **Sources:** CSI's 170 Google reviews (as mirrored by Trustindex), competitor review pages, and a parent checklist for choosing coaching
**Date of reviews:** May 2025 to April 2026 | **Compiled:** 13 October 2026

Confidence tags: [Certain] = seen directly in the sources; [Likely] = strong inference; [Guessing] = gap-filling.

## Executive summary

The uncomfortable finding comes first. **The two complaints with real substance in CSI's reviews are both about Physics teaching, and one of them alleges that students who complain are pressured into posting positive reviews.** [Certain] That's 2 of the 3 non-positive written reviews visible. It matters for JEE, where Physics carries a third of the marks.

The praise is consistent and matches the site's message: doubt-clearing, regular tests, and board and entrance teaching together. [Likely] But the review base has credibility problems. One review is posted under the name "CSI INSTITUTE", one reads like keyword-stuffed marketing copy, and 8 of the 20 visible reviews are stars with no text. Parents read reviews closely and notice these things.

## Key themes

### Theme 1: Personal attention and doubt-clearing are the main praise
- **Prevalence:** 5 of 12 written reviews. [Certain]
- **Summary:** Students describe teachers checking whether they've actually understood, solving doubts on the spot, and giving personal attention. One mentions small batch sizes.
- **Implication:** This is CSI's strongest proof point, and it's backed by students. It deserves a concrete promise on the site, such as "doubts solved the same day", rather than a general claim.

### Theme 2: Students name Double Teaching without being prompted
- **Prevalence:** 4 of 12. [Certain]
- **Summary:** Reviewers describe board and entrance teaching as separate but balanced, which is exactly what "Two hours. Two exams. One teacher." says. One praises the director's Biology teaching by name.
- **Implication:** [Likely] The homepage headline is grounded in what students actually experience. It isn't just marketing, so keep it.

### Theme 3: Regular tests and mocks are valued
- **Prevalence:** 5 of 12.
- **Summary:** Weekly tests that feel like the real exam, mock tests and study material all come up.
- **Implication:** The test calendar on the homepage supports this. It will be stronger once the institute confirms the real dates.

### Theme 4: Physics teaching is the weak point
- **Prevalence:** 2 of 12 overall, but 2 of the 3 non-positive reviews. [Certain]
- **Summary:** One student says Chemistry and Maths are good but Physics is poor, and calls the institute better for HSC board exams than for JEE or NEET. Another, after two years of classes, suggests the institute stop teaching Physics altogether.
- **Implication:** [Likely] This undermines the JEE page more than any design issue. The institute doesn't reply to the negative review, which makes it look worse.

### Theme 5: How credible the reviews look
**Observations** [Certain]:
- one review is posted under the name "CSI INSTITUTE";
- one review is heavy with search keywords;
- one reviewer has two reviews on the same day;
- one review alleges that staff pressure students into positive reviews;
- 9 of the 170 reviews (5%) are one-star, and most of them aren't visible;
- the institute replies to 63% of reviews, but not to the critical one.

**Interpretation** [Likely]: an admissions-season parent reading the latest reviews will see the Physics complaint first, because it's the newest, from April 2026.

### Theme 6: What nobody mentions
Fees, actual results and batch size beyond one mention. [Certain]

Competitor reviews complain about high fees and poor value (Aakash), weak phone counselling and confusing directions (Allen), and traffic at the branch (Allen). [Likely] Those are gaps CSI could claim: clear fees, a counsellor who actually calls back, and a quiet location on Hudkeshwar Road.

## Insights → opportunities

| Insight | Opportunity | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doubt-clearing is the most praised feature | A concrete promise on the site, such as "doubts cleared the same day, at the daily doubt hour" | High | Low |
| The Physics complaint is the newest review | The institute addresses the teaching itself; publicly, a calm owner reply and the Physics teacher named on the JEE page | High | Medium |
| A review is posted as "CSI INSTITUTE" | Ask Google to remove it, and stop any self-posted reviews | High (trust) | Low |
| Competitors are criticised over counselling | The "Book free counselling" button needs a promise behind it: who calls, and within how long | Medium | Low |
| Nobody talks about fees, and competitors are called expensive | Show a fee range or "what the fee includes" on the course pages [Guessing: needs the institute's agreement] | Medium | Low |
| CSI has 170 reviews, against 764–879 for the biggest rivals | Ask every passing batch for an honest review, with no pressure | Medium | Medium |

## Who the reviewers are

| Group | What they look like | What they need | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board-focused students | Value notes, board answer-writing and balance | Board marks *and* an entrance attempt | [Guessing] about half |
| Serious JEE aspirants | Judge each subject teacher separately | Strong Physics and Maths | Small but vocal |
| NEET students | Praise the Biology teaching and the director | NCERT depth, diagrams | [Guessing] about a third |

Parents rarely write reviews themselves. Students write them, and parents read them.

## Recommendations

1. **Deal with the Physics feedback before advertising JEE.** It comes from Theme 4. Paid ads would send JEE parents straight to a page whose newest Google review says not to come for JEE.
2. **Clean up review credibility.** It comes from Theme 5: remove the self-posted review, reply calmly to the critical one, and never collect reviews under pressure.
3. **Turn the praised things into specific promises on the site** (same-day doubts, the weekly test schedule) and put a promise behind the counselling button. This comes from Themes 1, 3 and 6.

## Questions for further research

- What do the 9 one-star reviews say? Only 20 of the 170 reviews were visible.
- Is the Physics complaint about one teacher or one batch, and is it still true?
- Why did families who enquired choose another institute? A 5-question phone call with 10 lost leads would answer this better than any amount of reading reviews.

## Methodology notes

- The sample is very small: 12 written reviews out of 170. Every count above is a signal, not a statistic.
- Justdial and NagpurOnline wouldn't load. UrbanPro and ProvenExpert listings have no reviews. VLocal shows a 4.6 rating but no review text.
- Competitor evidence is thin and partly old (the Aakash reviews are from 2018).
- Reviews over-represent extremes, and this source mostly shows the newest ones.
- Reviews are paraphrased, not quoted, and reviewers are not named.
